## Spec
Remove Accor from executable hotel selection; explicitly deny its domains in the machine-readable site-policy registry. Replace assumptions about Accor/Hilton/rail/Google Flights being authorized with dated reviews of actual applicable terms for every site/domain used, including organizer -> registration platform, hotel booking engines and transport result/carrier redirects. Review terms before any live navigation. Registry entries must identify host/path and purpose scope, official terms URLs, reviewed_at/expires_at, reviewed sections/short justification, automation decision, limited in-session screenshot/live-view scope, explicitly permitted navigation hosts, and registration account/captcha observations with source/date. Unknown, expired, prohibited or materially ambiguous scopes fail closed. Never use robots.txt as authorization or broadly allow all subdomains/platform customers from one reviewed race. Keep registration access observations distinct from policy permission. Implement exactly evaluate_url and registration_info from the shared notes with offline normal/empty/error tests.

Select compliant alternatives on evidence, not popularity: generic hotel and transport routing should prefer reviewed public deep-link flows that show inventory for the selected city/dates without search-form submission, login, consent or captcha. No race/city special-case executable path; configurable reviewed site adapters may cover different regions, and unsupported destinations must block honestly rather than silently fall back to Accor/Omio/a homepage. Review read-only browser automation and screenshot scope separately; screenshot-only removes H trace publication but does not waive a site's copyright/terms. If no compliant option exists, record the gap and do not invent authorization or success.

Preserve build_task(trip)->dict/ValueError and existing keys. Populate the pinned expected object, prefer officially linked deep URLs encoding confirmed arrival/departure dates, travelers and destination, and include explicit date verification before proceeding. Never use today/default UI dates, swap check-in/check-out, silently change a race date or substitute inventory outside the requested stay. Unknown date/city, invalid travel order, impossible/stale edition or missing required trip fields must reject/block. Use proper URL encoding and ordinary public date/route query parameters, no session tokens. Raise requested step budgets to 80 for nontrivial flows while retaining bounded instructions and no guardrail weakening.

Registration must select the sourced race edition and distance before stopping at its first personal-data page, untouched. Account walls/lottery-only/closed/sold-out/captcha/earlier-consent remain blocked; there is no automatic account creation or platform-name guarantee. Hotel must reach a selected available room AND rate showing correct stay, party and total price before guest data/payment. Transport must reach selected viable outbound AND return/fare with outbound arrival by the planned arrival day, and correct return/party/price before passenger data/payment. Unselected flight results or a public homepage cannot be ready. Define site-specific success/blocked evidence in instructions, pass allowed navigation hosts from reviewed policy, and explicitly stop on any unreviewed redirect/domain or forbidden form submission.

Update owned evidence docs to replace the R1 'assumed' claims with exactly what was reviewed/observed versus unverified. Coordinate safe live task smokes only AFTER R2-03's no-share runtime is available (no bypassing its gate). Verify at least two countries and one non-Milan case using sourced future dates; include a date-mismatch negative case offline, and report unavailable inventory/horizons honestly. Do not edit hclient/providers/guide/core/UI or their tests.
