# T02 — Framing extraction and clarification

## Goal
Build a guided proposing chatbot, not a form. Accept vague wishes such as
« je veux courir un marathon au printemps en Italie » or « le marathon de Milan ».
Propose concrete races, editions, distances and official dates through a discovery
interface connected to Linkup by T03; use labelled synthetic candidates in T02.
The user must not need to know the date/edition. Fill sourced fields yourself,
suggest editable origin/nights/budget defaults, and distinguish defaults from facts.
Ask at most one focused question at a time with clickable suggestion chips and
free-text corrections. Show sourced option/plan cards and explicit confirmation
before H preparation. Discovery can propose other races; execution stays Milan.
Handle no matches, unknown/conflicting dates and errors without inventing values.
Document guided-turn/discovery contracts for T03. Split static JS/CSS if useful;
T08 owns final visual polish. Test vague/name-only input, chips, defaults,
corrections, empty input/results and discovery errors; keep payment guards.
