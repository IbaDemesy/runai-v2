# R1-04 — Guided proposing conversation engine (FR/EN), one question at a time with chips, synthetic fake discovery

## Goal
Build an isolated French/English guided conversation engine with injectable
discovery and clearly labelled synthetic fake candidates. Parse vague wishes
or race names without requiring dates; propose races and official sourced dates
when discovery provides them. Ask at most one focused question per turn, offer
clickable suggestions and editable origin/nights/budget defaults, and support
free-text corrections or accepting defaults in one click. Keep suggested values
distinct from sourced facts. Produce selected race/plan and confirmation
readiness for R1-01; never start H agents itself. Empty/conflicting discovery,
unknown dates, invalid input and errors need useful single-question recovery.
Publish interface/state details in the scoped handoff, not CONTRACTS.md.
Test FR/EN vague/name-only wishes, chips, defaults, corrections and error paths.

## Dependencies and merge order
Independent implementation/merge stage; no prerequisite merge.
Merge stage 1. Component interfaces may be coordinated in parallel;
R1-01 integrates only after stage 1 lands. R1-07 follows R1-01.

## Guardrails
Follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md) and owner feedback.
Milan is one evaluation case, never a hard-coded path. No payment, booking,
accounts, terms/consent acceptance, medical declarations, form submission,
personal-data entry or captcha bypass. Cookie banners may only be dismissed
without accepting consent. Stop earlier than payment if any boundary appears.
Read keys from environment only; no secrets, tokens, private H URLs, emails or
other personal data in code, fixtures, logs or evidence. Never invent results
or live footage. Demo/replay must be visibly synthetic; missing live keys/modules
must fail explicitly, never fall back to fake success.
